A letter from the heart

Happy Birthday Mom

Dear Mom, ❤️

I just want to take a moment to say how incredibly grateful I am to have you as my mother. Looking back over all the years of my childhood I can see just how lucky I've been to have been brought up by such a patient, loving and caring person. Thank you for loving me when I was difficult to understand, thank you for being so patient with me when I was wrong and thank you for never giving up on me when things were tough.

Thanks for teaching me how to respect people, how to be a better person and how to live life with the right values. You were always there to steer me when I was lost, to remind me what was right and to help me find my way when life got tough. Your love and your guidance have been the very thing that has made me who I am today and I know that a huge amount of the good in me is down to everything you've taught me.

Thank you for looking after me, for supporting my dreams, for believing in me and for helping me to achieve them. I know that being a Mom is no easy thing and there are no doubt countless sacrifices you've made for me that I'll never really be able to put into words. I don't always say it and I don't always show it, but I do appreciate everything you've ever done for me, from the tiniest little thing to the biggest sacrifice you've ever made out of love.

Mom, I just want you to know that I am absolutely proud to be your son. If there's one thing I'll always be thankful for in life it's that I was given a mother like you. You're so much more than just my mother. You're my mentor, my safe place, my source of inspiration and one of the greatest gifts that life has ever given me. Whatever age I get to, whatever life throws at me, I'll always be your son, the son you brought up with such love and such care.

I know I've still got so much to learn and I know I'm not always the perfect son, but I promise to keep on growing into the man you can be proud of. I hope one day I can give back a little bit of the love and happiness you've given me over the years.

Thanks for being my Mom, for being there for me through my childhood, for being there as I've grown up and for loving me in a way that words can't really express. I'll never be able to repay you for all that you've done for me, but I'll carry your love and all the things you've taught me with me all the way through.

I love you so much Mommy. Thank you for being my first teacher, my life long teacher and the person I will always be so grateful to have as my Mom. I am and will always be so proud to be your son. ❤️

With all my love
